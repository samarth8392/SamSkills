# Global Developmental Delay

> **Spark note:** A placeholder diagnosis for children **under 5** who miss milestones in several areas of intellectual functioning but cannot yet be tested systematically, so IDD severity cannot be set. It must be **reassessed** — it is not a final answer. Don't confuse it with unspecified IDD (F79), which is for **over 5** when testing is blocked by sensory/physical/behavioural barriers (p. 142).

## Criteria at a glance (p. 142)
- Age **<5 years**.
- Fails to meet expected developmental milestones in **several areas** of intellectual functioning.
- Unable to undergo systematic assessment of intellectual functioning (including being too young for standardized testing) → clinical severity cannot be reliably assessed.
- **Requires reassessment** after a period of time.

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| F88 | Global developmental delay |
- Note: F88 is also the code for Other specified neurodevelopmental disorder.

## Diagnostic features & clinical pearls
- Some children <5 who will eventually meet IDD criteria first meet GDD criteria (p. 139).
- It is common practice to delay an IDD diagnosis in infants/young children until after an appropriate course of intervention (p. 139).
- GDD is an exclusion in language disorder, SPCD and ASD (Criterion E) — those diagnoses require deficits beyond the global delay.

## Differential diagnosis
- **IDD:** once standardized IQ and adaptive testing are possible and severity can be set.
- **Unspecified IDD (F79):** age >5, testing blocked by blindness, prelingual deafness, locomotor disability, severe problem behaviour or co-occurring mental disorder.
- **Language disorder / ASD / SPCD:** diagnose only if the specific deficits exceed the global delay.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Child's age (<5) and the milestone domains delayed (motor, language, social, cognitive/problem-solving, self-help), with expected vs observed ages.
- Why standardized intellectual testing is not yet feasible.
- Hearing/vision screening and medical/genetic workup status.
- Planned reassessment interval and interventions in place (early intervention, speech, OT).
- Phrases: "no single words at 18 months; does not point to request"; "delays across language, fine motor and play skills"; "too young for reliable standardized IQ testing; reassess at age __".
