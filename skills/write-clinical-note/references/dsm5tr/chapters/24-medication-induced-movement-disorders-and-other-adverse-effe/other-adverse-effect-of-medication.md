# Other Adverse Effect of Medication (T50.905_)

> **Spark note:** An **optional** code for **non-movement** medication side effects that have become a **main focus of clinical attention**. The text's examples are severe hypotension, cardiac arrhythmias and priapism (p. 1083).

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| T50.905A | Initial encounter |
| T50.905D | Subsequent encounter |
| T50.905S | Sequelae |

## When to use
- The side effect itself drives the visit or treatment decisions (e.g., orthostatic hypotension leading to a med change).
- Not for movement side effects; use the specific G codes in this chapter.
- Not for antidepressant discontinuation (T43.205_).

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Medication, dose, onset relative to start or change, objective data (BP readings, ECG findings, physical sx).
- Why it is a main focus: med hold/change, medical referral, safety risk.
- Impact on adherence and on the underlying mental disorder's treatment plan.
