# G25.79 Other Medication-Induced Movement Disorder

> **Spark note:** A residual code for medication-induced movement problems that **don't fit** MIP, NMS, acute dystonia, acute akathisia, TD, tardive dystonia/akathisia or postural tremor (p. 1081).

## When to use (text examples)
1. A presentation **resembling NMS** caused by a medication **other than** an antipsychotic/DRBA.
2. **Other medication-induced tardive conditions** (tardive movement types not named elsewhere).

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| G25.79 | Other Medication-Induced Movement Disorder |

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Name the movement phenomenology and the suspected agent, with a timeline.
- Say which specific categories were considered and why they don't fit (e.g., "NMS-like syndrome but no DRBA exposure").
- Safety and medical coordination as indicated.
