# Avoidant Personality Disorder (Cluster C)

> **Spark note:** Pervasive social inhibition driven by feeling inadequate and hypersensitive to criticism — the
> person *wants* connection but withdraws until certain of acceptance. Heavy overlap with social anxiety disorder
> (possibly a more severe/entrenched variant), but they can occur separately and both may be diagnosed. Don't
> pathologize developmentally normal shyness in youth.

## Criteria at a glance (p. 1017–1018)
Pervasive social inhibition, inadequacy feelings, hypersensitivity to negative evaluation; by early adulthood; multiple
contexts; **≥4 of 7**:
1. Avoids occupational activities with significant interpersonal contact (fear of criticism, disapproval, rejection)
2. Unwilling to get involved unless certain of being liked
3. Restraint in intimate relationships from fear of shame/ridicule
4. Preoccupied with criticism or rejection in social situations
5. Inhibited in new interpersonal situations due to inadequacy feelings
6. Sees self as socially inept, unappealing, or inferior
7. Unusually reluctant to take personal risks or try new activities for fear of embarrassment

## Specifiers & severity
- None.

## Diagnostic features & clinical pearls (p. 1018–1019)
- Declines promotions for fear of coworker criticism; avoids group activities.
- Others presumed critical until "stringent tests" prove otherwise; intimacy possible only with assurance of uncritical acceptance.
- Very low threshold for detecting criticism; shy, quiet, "invisible"; says nothing for fear it will be "wrong"; misreads neutral
  cues as mocking.
- Longs to participate socially but fears entrusting welfare to others.
- Self-doubt may peak with strangers **or** with repeated interaction where disclosure would normally deepen — growing
  conviction over weeks/months that colleagues view them as inferior → resignation; repeated job changes.
- Exaggerates ordinary dangers; restricted lifestyle from need for certainty.

## Associated features (p. 1019)
- Vigilant scanning of others' expressions; misreading confirms self-doubt; described as shy, timid, lonely, isolated.
- Major impairment in social and occupational functioning; small support network.
- Desires affection; fantasizes idealized relationships.
- Insecure attachment ("preoccupied" or "fearful"); fear of being devalued → passivity, anger, or fear.

## Prevalence · Development & course · Risk & prognostic factors
- NCS-R 5.2%; NESARC 2.4%; median of 6 studies 2.1% (p. 1019).
- Starts in infancy/childhood with shyness, isolation, fear of strangers/new situations; most shy children improve, but those who
  develop AvPD become **more** shy/avoidant in adolescence/early adulthood.
- Tends to become less evident or remit with age in adults; >65 y prevalence ~0.8% (p. 1020).
- Use great caution in children/adolescents.

## Culture- & sex/gender-related issues (p. 1020)
- Cultural groups vary in viewing diffidence/avoidance as appropriate; avoidance may stem from acculturation problems after migration.
- In some contexts marked avoidance follows "loss of face" or failure to meet major life goals, with deliberate minimization of
  contact to preserve harmony or avoid offense — not temperamental shyness.
- Somewhat more common in women in community surveys (small but consistent).

## Suicide risk
Not specifically addressed.

## Differential diagnosis (p. 1020–1021)
- **Social anxiety disorder:** large overlap; may be different manifestations of similar problems, or AvPD more severe. AvPD has
  more pervasive, entrenched negative self-concept/inferiority; AvPD often occurs without SAD; separate risk factors exist.
- **Agoraphobia:** avoidance motivated by fear of panic or physical harm; often co-occur.
- **Dependent PD:** both inadequacy, criticism sensitivity, need for reassurance, unassertiveness; AvPD avoids proximity
  (fear of rejection/humiliation), DPD seeks proximity (fear of being left to fend for self). Often co-occur.
- **Schizoid/schizotypal PD:** content with/prefer isolation; AvPD wants relationships and feels loneliness deeply.
- **Paranoid PD:** both reluctant to confide; AvPD from fear of humiliation/inadequacy, not others' malice.
- **Traits:** only inflexible, maladaptive, impairing traits qualify.
- **Personality change due to AMC; substance use disorders.**

## Comorbidity (p. 1021)
- Depressive and anxiety disorders, especially social anxiety disorder; schizoid PD.
- SUD rates elevated similar to generalized social anxiety disorder.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
These are assessment prompts, not observed findings. Record only assessed/reported evidence, attribute its source, and preserve uncertainty; an MSE list does not describe every person with the diagnosis.

- **1/7:** declined promotions, jobs, classes, or activities and the stated reason (fear of criticism/embarrassment).
- **2/3:** relationship history — only with assured acceptance; withholding personal info in intimate relationships.
- **4/5/6:** client's self-description ("I'm boring/inferior"), rumination about past criticism, freezing in new situations.
- Record **desire** for relationships (separates from schizoid).
- **MSE:** anxious, inhibited, soft-spoken, limited eye contact, self-deprecating content, sensitivity to clinician's reactions;
  no psychotic features.
- **Rule-outs:** SAD (which can involve broad social fears, not just performance); examine enduring negative self-concept, course, and independent criteria for both, agoraphobia
  (panic-related avoidance), depressive episode (time-limited withdrawal), cultural "loss of face" context.
- Note onset (shyness worsened through adolescence) and occupational consequences (job changes, underemployment).
