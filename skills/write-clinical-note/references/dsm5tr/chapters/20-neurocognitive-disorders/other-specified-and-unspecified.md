# Other Specified Delirium, Unspecified Delirium, and Unspecified Neurocognitive Disorder

> **Spark note:** The residual delirium categories distinguish whether a specific reason for falling short is communicated. Unspecified NCD also accommodates insufficient certainty about etiology. These labels still require clinically meaningful distress or impairment. (pp. 915, 978)

## Criteria at a glance
- **Other specified delirium (R41.0):** delirium-like symptoms predominate and impair function/cause distress, but no specific NCD diagnosis is fully met. State the particular reason. The source's example is **subsyndromal delirium**: attention, higher-order thinking and circadian disturbance below the delirium threshold.
- **Unspecified delirium (R41.0):** analogous clinically significant presentation, with no stated reason for the unmet criteria; limited information in emergency settings is an example.
- **Unspecified NCD (R41.9):** clinically important characteristic cognitive symptoms with no fully established specific chapter diagnosis, used when a sufficiently certain etiological attribution cannot be made. The source applies R41.9 to unspecified major or mild NCD. (pp. 915, 978)

## Specifiers & severity
For unspecified NCD, record with/without behavioral disturbance in words. Do not infer an etiology or add codes for merely presumed causes. No special residual-delirium severity scale is given. (p. 978)

## Suicide risk
Not specifically addressed in these residual sections.

## Differential diagnosis
Reassess full delirium, major/mild NCD and their causes as evidence becomes available. A residual diagnosis does not establish a benign process or replace assessment of altered attention/arousal and physiological contributors. For full delirium versus NCD use the chapter's temporal, attention and baseline-function distinctions. (pp. 914–915, 978)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record actual symptoms, baseline change, time course, objective findings and functional impact.
- For other specified delirium, state exactly which requirement is below threshold and why.
- For unspecified labels, document missing information or attribution uncertainty and the specific diagnoses considered.
