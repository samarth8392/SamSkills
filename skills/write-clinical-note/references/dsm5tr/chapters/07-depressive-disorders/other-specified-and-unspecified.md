# Other Specified Depressive Disorder · Unspecified Depressive Disorder · Unspecified Mood Disorder

> **Spark note:** Residual categories for **clinically significant depressive presentations** that
> fall short of full criteria for any depressive disorder **and** for adjustment disorder with depressed mood (or with
> mixed anxiety and depressed mood). **OSDD (F32.89)** = you state the reason. **UDD (F32.A)** = you don't, or can't (e.g., ED).
> **Unspecified mood disorder (F39)**, new in DSM-5-TR, covers cases where you can't yet tell bipolar-spectrum from depressive
> (e.g., acute agitation). Common error: using these when adjustment disorder fits better.

## Criteria at a glance
**Shared entry criteria (OSDD and UDD)** (pp. 340–341):
- Depressive symptoms **predominate** and cause clinically significant distress or impairment.
- Full criteria **not met** for any depressive disorder in the chapter.
- Criteria **not met** for adjustment disorder with depressed mood or with mixed anxiety and depressed mood.

### Other specified depressive disorder (F32.89)
Record "other specified depressive disorder" + the **specific reason** (e.g., "short-duration depressive episode").
Example presentations:
1. **Recurrent brief depression:** depressed mood **plus ≥4 other** depressive symptoms together for **2–13 days**, **at least monthly**, for **≥12 consecutive months**, not tied to the menstrual cycle. The person has **never** met criteria for another depressive or bipolar disorder and does not currently meet active or residual criteria for a psychotic disorder.
2. **Short-duration depressive episode (4–13 days):** depressed affect **plus ≥4 of the other 8** MDE symptoms, with distress or impairment, lasting **>4 and <14 days**. Never met other depressive or bipolar criteria; no active/residual psychotic disorder; not recurrent brief depression.
3. **Depressive episode with insufficient symptoms:** depressed affect **plus ≥1 of the other 8** MDE symptoms, with distress or impairment, lasting **≥2 weeks**. Never met other depressive or bipolar criteria; no active/residual psychotic disorder; does not meet criteria for **mixed anxiety and depressive disorder** symptoms.
4. **MDE superimposed on schizophrenia, schizophreniform disorder, delusional disorder, or other specified/unspecified psychotic disorder.** *Not* for MDEs that are part of **schizoaffective disorder** (no added OSDD).

Other uses mentioned elsewhere in the chapter:
- Depressed mood with impairment that falls short on **duration or severity** for an MDE (MDD differential, p. 320).
- **Chronic depressive symptoms >2 years** that miss PDD criteria and have never met MDE criteria (PDD differential, p. 324).
- **Insufficient evidence** to decide among substance-induced, due to AMC, or independent (SMIDD differential, p. 336). OSDD or UDD.

### Unspecified depressive disorder (F32.A)
- Same entry criteria. The clinician **chooses not to specify** why criteria aren't met, **or there is insufficient information** (e.g., emergency room).

### Unspecified mood disorder (F39)
- Mood-disorder symptoms with distress or impairment that, **at the time of evaluation**, do not meet full criteria for any bipolar **or** depressive disorder **and** where it is **hard to choose between unspecified bipolar and related disorder and unspecified depressive disorder** (e.g., **acute agitation**).

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| F32.89 | Other specified depressive disorder (+ reason, e.g., "recurrent brief depression") |
| F32.A | Unspecified depressive disorder |
| F39 | Unspecified mood disorder (bipolar vs depressive undeterminable) |

Recording examples: "F32.89 other specified depressive disorder, short-duration depressive episode"; "F32.89 other specified depressive disorder, major depressive episode superimposed on schizophrenia" (listed with the schizophrenia code).

## Diagnostic features & clinical pearls
- **Prefer OSDD over UDD whenever you can name the reason.** It carries more clinical information.
- Most named OSDD presentations require that the person has **never met criteria for any other depressive or bipolar disorder**. A prior MDE makes them inapplicable.
- **Recurrent brief depression vs short-duration episode:** RBD is a recurring monthly pattern over a year (2–13 days each); the short-duration episode is a single 4–13-day episode.
- **Insufficient-symptom episode:** has the full 2-week duration but **only 2–4 total symptoms** (needs ≥5 for an MDE).
- **F39** is a holding diagnosis for acute or undifferentiated mood presentations. **Revise it** once polarity is clear.
- A loss reaction, or normal sadness, with sub-MDE impairment: consider adjustment disorder first (excluded from these categories if met), then OSDD.

## Differential diagnosis
- **MDD:** needs ≥5/9 symptoms for ≥2 weeks.
- **PDD:** needs ≥2 years with ≥2 of 6 symptoms.
- **Adjustment disorder with depressed mood / with mixed anxiety and depressed mood:** if met, use it instead of OSDD or UDD.
- **Schizoaffective disorder:** MDEs within it do not get an extra OSDD.
- **Unspecified bipolar and related disorder:** if any hypomanic/manic features are evident, consider bipolar residual categories. Use F39 only when polarity can't be determined.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- **State which criteria are and aren't met**, e.g. "depressed mood + insomnia + poor concentration for 3 weeks (3/9 symptoms; MDE threshold not met); stressor absent so adjustment disorder not applicable → OSDD, depressive episode with insufficient symptoms."
- **For RBD:** log episode dates and lengths (2–13 days), frequency (≥monthly), span (≥12 months) and absence of menstrual linkage.
- **For short-duration episodes:** exact day count (4–13) and symptom list (depressed affect + ≥4).
- **Lifetime screen:** no prior depressive, bipolar or psychotic disorder criteria (required for examples 1–3).
- **Adjustment disorder rule-out:** document presence or absence of an identifiable stressor and its timing.
- **UDD / F39:** document why specification wasn't possible (limited history, acute presentation, collateral unavailable, agitation precluding interview) and the plan to re-evaluate.
- **Safety:** document SI screening regardless of the subthreshold status.
