# Unspecified Mood Disorder

> **Spark note:** A **DSM-5-TR addition** (F39) for cross-class uncertainty. Mood symptoms predominate and impair, but at the time of evaluation they don't meet criteria for any bipolar **or** depressive disorder, **and** it is hard to choose between *unspecified bipolar and related disorder* and *unspecified depressive disorder*. The text's example is **acute agitation**. It appears in both the bipolar and depressive chapters.

## Criteria at a glance (pp. 341–342)
- Symptoms characteristic of a **mood disorder** predominate and cause clinically significant distress or impairment.
- At the time of evaluation, full criteria are **not met** for any disorder in the bipolar **or** depressive classes.
- It is **difficult to choose** between unspecified bipolar and related disorder (F31.9) and unspecified depressive disorder (F32.A).

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| **F39** | Unspecified mood disorder |

## Diagnostic features & clinical pearls
- This is a placeholder for when polarity is unclear. Typical settings: ER or inpatient admission with agitation, limited history, or no informants.
- If the presentation is clearly bipolar-type, use F31.89/F31.9. If clearly depressive-type, use the depressive chapter's other specified/unspecified codes.
- Revisit once history or collateral clarifies polarity.

## Differential diagnosis
- **Unspecified bipolar and related disorder:** use it when the symptoms are recognizably bipolar-spectrum (elevation, ↑ energy).
- **Unspecified depressive disorder:** use it when the symptoms are recognizably depressive without elevation.
- Substance/medication-induced or medical-condition-related mood disorders should be considered first when etiology is evident.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Describe the mood symptoms observed (e.g., agitation, irritability, labile affect, dysphoria) and the resulting impairment.
- State **why polarity can't be determined** (e.g., "acutely agitated, unable to give history; no collateral available; unclear whether presentation reflects mixed/manic vs agitated depressive state").
- Document what remains to be clarified (prior episodes, substance screen, collateral) and the plan for reassessment.
- Record the risk assessment (agitation, harm to self or others) regardless of diagnostic uncertainty.
