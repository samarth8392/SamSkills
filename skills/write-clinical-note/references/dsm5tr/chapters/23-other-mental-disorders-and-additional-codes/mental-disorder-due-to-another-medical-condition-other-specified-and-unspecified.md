# Other Specified / Unspecified Mental Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition

> **Spark note:** Use these when psychiatric symptoms are judged to be the **direct physiological result** of a medical condition but do not fit any of the specific "due to another medical condition" categories found elsewhere in Section II. Classic example: dissociative symptoms from complex partial seizures. The rule people miss is that the **medical condition must be coded first**, immediately before F06.8/F09.

## Criteria at a glance
Both categories require all of the following:
- Symptoms typical of a mental disorder due to AMC **predominate**.
- They cause clinically significant distress or impairment (social, occupational, other).
- Full criteria for any specific mental disorder attributable to AMC are **not** met.
- The symptoms are a direct physiological consequence of the medical condition (p. 1065).

| | F06.8 Other specified | F09 Unspecified |
|---|---|---|
| Reason criteria unmet | Clinician records it (the symptom manifestation) | Not recorded; includes insufficient info (e.g., ED) |
| Recording | Name with medical condition inserted + manifestation | Name with medical condition inserted |
| Example (p. 1066-1067) | G40.209 complex partial seizures; F06.8 other specified mental disorder due to complex partial seizures, dissociative symptoms | G40.209 complex partial seizures; F09 unspecified mental disorder due to complex partial seizures |

Example presentation the text gives for other specified: **dissociative symptoms**, e.g., in the context of complex partial seizures.

## Coding
| Code | Use when | Recording order |
|---|---|---|
| (medical code) | Always, e.g., B20 HIV disease, G40.209 complex partial seizures | **First**, immediately before the F code |
| F06.8 | Due-to-AMC picture, reason specified | Second; write "...due to [condition], [manifestation]" |
| F09 | Due-to-AMC picture, reason not specified / insufficient info | Second; write "...due to [condition]" |

## Diagnostic features & clinical pearls
- These are **not** a substitute for the specific due-to-AMC diagnoses (e.g., psychotic disorder due to AMC, anxiety disorder due to AMC). Check those first.
- F09 suits the ED or consultation-liaison setting when the medical link is clear but the phenomenology hasn't been characterized.
- Name the actual medical condition. Never write "another medical condition" literally.

## Differential diagnosis
- **Specific due-to-AMC disorders**: use when their criteria are met.
- **Delirium / neurocognitive disorders**: disturbance of attention/awareness or cognitive decline points to these.
- **Substance/medication-induced disorders**: the cause is a drug, not the disease.
- **F99 other specified/unspecified mental disorder**: no established physiological link to a medical condition.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Medical condition: diagnosis, source (records, PCP/neurology), and code; list first.
- Evidence of etiological link: temporal onset with illness, course tracking illness activity, atypical features for a primary psychiatric dx, exam/lab/EEG/imaging findings.
- Symptom description (e.g., episodic depersonalization tied to seizure activity) and why no specific due-to-AMC category fits.
- Distress/impairment, e.g., "missed work 3 days/month due to episodes; distressed by gaps in awareness."
- Rule-outs to document: delirium (attention/awareness intact?), substance/medication effects, primary dissociative/psychiatric disorder.
- Sample wording: "Symptoms judged to be a direct physiological consequence of [condition] per [evidence]; they do not meet criteria for a specific mental disorder due to AMC; recorded as F06.8 other specified mental disorder due to [condition], [manifestation]."
