# Other Specified and Unspecified Somatic Symptom and Related Disorders

> **Spark note:** These categories require a characteristic, distressing or impairing presentation without full criteria for a chapter disorder. Other specified states the reason; unspecified is reserved here for decidedly unusual situations with insufficient information (pp. 541–542).

## Criteria at a glance
- Chapter-characteristic symptoms predominate, with clinically significant distress/impairment.
- Full criteria for a specific chapter disorder are not met.
- **Other specified:** document the particular subthreshold pattern.
- **Unspecified:** the source restricts use to unusual circumstances where diagnostic information is insufficient (pp. 541–542).

## Specifiers & severity
Examples given (p. 541):
1. **Brief somatic symptom disorder:** <6 months.
2. **Brief illness anxiety disorder:** <6 months.
3. **Illness anxiety without excessive behaviors/avoidance:** IAD criterion D not met.
4. **Pseudocyesis:** belief in pregnancy accompanied by objective signs and reported pregnancy symptoms.
No separate formal severity or remission scale.

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| F45.8 | Other specified; append the reason/presentation |
| F45.9 | Unspecified; unusual insufficient-information situations |

## Diagnostic features & clinical pearls
Pseudocyesis includes bodily signs; belief alone does not establish it. Unexplained medical symptoms alone do not establish a mental disorder (pp. 516–517, 541).

## Suicide risk
Not specifically addressed.

## Differential diagnosis
- **Full SSD/IAD:** examine duration and all required positive features before selecting a residual category.
- **Medical condition/pregnancy:** establish medical findings; do not infer pseudocyesis from a report alone.
- **Psychotic disorder:** evaluate delusional conviction and broader syndrome when pregnancy belief lacks the described somatic presentation; the residual passage gives no standalone psychosis algorithm (pp. 517, 541).

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record characteristic symptoms, functional effect, and exactly which full criterion is absent.
- For brief presentations, give onset and elapsed duration rather than rounding to six months.
- For pseudocyesis, separate belief, reported symptoms, observed signs and medical assessment.
- For F45.9, explain what information is missing and why this unusual residual use is necessary; identify follow-up needs.
