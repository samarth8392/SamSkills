# Borderline Personality Disorder — Alternative Model

> **Spark note:** Unstable identity, goals, affect, and close relationships with impulsivity, dangerous behavior, or hostility. This is the Section III alternative-model profile, not the Section II criteria set.

## Proposed criteria at a glance
**A:** ≥moderate personality-functioning impairment, with characteristic difficulty in ≥2 of these four areas:

- Identity: impoverished/unstable self-image, self-criticism, emptiness, or stress-linked dissociation.
- Self-direction: unstable aspirations, values, or plans.
- Empathy: interpersonal sensitivity and negative bias interfere with understanding others.
- Intimacy: intense conflicted ties, abandonment fears, idealization/devaluation, and alternating closeness/withdrawal.

**B:** ≥4 of 7, including ≥1 of impulsivity, risk taking, hostility: emotional lability, anxiousness, separation insecurity, depressivity, impulsivity, risk taking, hostility.

**C–G:** All [general alternative-model criteria](general-model-and-assessment.md) also apply: pervasiveness/inflexibility, relative stability with adolescent/early-adult onset, and exclusion of other mental disorders, substance/medical causes, and normative developmental/sociocultural explanations.

## Specifiers & severity
Additional traits, including cognitive/perceptual dysregulation, and LPFS severity may be specified.

## Coding and model boundaries
No distinct ICD code is supplied for this proposed alternative profile. Establish and code an official Section II diagnosis using its own criteria; do not combine trait thresholds here with Section II counts.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Assess temporal relation to interpersonal events, pervasive pattern versus mood episodes, and reported self-injury/suicidality directly. Do not infer intent from the label or assume that all people with these traits self-injure.
- Record specific examples for ≥2 functioning areas and each counted trait; identify duration, settings, baseline, impact, sources, and contrary evidence.
- Document general exclusions and the model used. Additional traits are descriptive specifiers and cannot substitute for a required facet.
- This chapter does not provide separate profile-specific epidemiology, course, or comprehensive comorbidity sections.
