# Avoidant Personality Disorder — Alternative Model

> **Spark note:** Social inhibition and avoidance driven by inadequacy and fear of criticism or rejection. This is the Section III alternative-model profile, not the Section II criteria set.

## Proposed criteria at a glance
**A:** ≥moderate personality-functioning impairment, with characteristic difficulty in ≥2 of these four areas:

- Identity: low esteem, shame, sense of inferiority or social ineptitude.
- Self-direction: unrealistically demanding standards inhibit goals, risks, and new social activity.
- Empathy: anticipated criticism distorts interpretation of others.
- Intimacy: fear of ridicule/rejection restricts closeness and mutuality.

**B:** ≥3 of 4, including anxiousness: anxiousness, withdrawal, anhedonia, intimacy avoidance.

**C–G:** All [general alternative-model criteria](general-model-and-assessment.md) also apply: pervasiveness/inflexibility, relative stability with adolescent/early-adult onset, and exclusion of other mental disorders, substance/medical causes, and normative developmental/sociocultural explanations.

## Specifiers & severity
Additional Negative Affectivity facets such as depressivity, separation insecurity, submissiveness, suspiciousness, or hostility and LPFS severity can be recorded.

## Coding and model boundaries
No distinct ICD code is supplied for this proposed alternative profile. Establish and code an official Section II diagnosis using its own criteria; do not combine trait thresholds here with Section II counts.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Clarify fear-driven avoidance versus preference for solitude, pervasive dysfunction versus an anxiety episode, and the impact of actual exclusion or discrimination. Record desires for connection and examples of inhibited goals.
- Record specific examples for ≥2 functioning areas and each counted trait; identify duration, settings, baseline, impact, sources, and contrary evidence.
- Document general exclusions and the model used. Additional traits are descriptive specifiers and cannot substitute for a required facet.
- This chapter does not provide separate profile-specific epidemiology, course, or comprehensive comorbidity sections.
