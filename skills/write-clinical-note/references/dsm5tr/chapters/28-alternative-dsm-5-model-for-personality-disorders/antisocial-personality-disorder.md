# Antisocial Personality Disorder — Alternative Model

> **Spark note:** Failure to meet lawful/ethical standards with egocentric, exploitative, callous relating. This is the Section III alternative-model profile, not the Section II criteria set.

## Proposed criteria at a glance
**A:** ≥moderate personality-functioning impairment, with characteristic difficulty in ≥2 of these four areas:

- Identity: esteem tied to gain, dominance, or pleasure.
- Self-direction: personal gratification governs goals; prosocial standards are absent.
- Empathy: little concern for suffering or remorse.
- Intimacy: exploitation, coercion, dominance, or deceit displaces mutual closeness.

**B:** ≥6 of 7: manipulativeness, callousness, deceitfulness, hostility, risk taking, impulsivity, irresponsibility. Age must be ≥18.

**C–G:** All [general alternative-model criteria](general-model-and-assessment.md) also apply: pervasiveness/inflexibility, relative stability with adolescent/early-adult onset, and exclusion of other mental disorders, substance/medical causes, and normative developmental/sociocultural explanations.

## Specifiers & severity
With psychopathic features: low anxiousness, low withdrawal, and high attention seeking describe a socially bold, relatively fearless variant. Additional traits and LPFS severity can be specified.

## Coding and model boundaries
No distinct ICD code is supplied for this proposed alternative profile. Establish and code an official Section II diagnosis using its own criteria; do not combine trait thresholds here with Section II counts.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- The alternative algorithm does not reproduce Section II's conduct-disorder-before-15 requirement; do not transfer either system's criteria into the other. Document actual harm, remorse, obligations, coercive behavior, developmental history, and possible substance or episodic causes.
- Record specific examples for ≥2 functioning areas and each counted trait; identify duration, settings, baseline, impact, sources, and contrary evidence.
- Document general exclusions and the model used. Additional traits are descriptive specifiers and cannot substitute for a required facet.
- This chapter does not provide separate profile-specific epidemiology, course, or comprehensive comorbidity sections.
