# Personality Disorder—Trait Specified

> **Spark note:** PD-TS describes alternative-model personality disorder whose pattern does not match one of the six named profiles. It still requires the general personality-disorder threshold; elevated traits alone are insufficient.

## Proposed criteria at a glance
- **A:** ≥moderate personality-functioning impairment with difficulty in ≥2 areas: identity, self-direction, empathy, intimacy.
- **B:** ≥1 pathological domain or specific facet. Consider **all five** domains: Negative Affectivity, Detachment, Antagonism, Disinhibition, Psychoticism.
- **C–G:** [General alternative-model requirements](general-model-and-assessment.md) apply in full. (pp. 1157–1158)

## When the profile applies
Use within the alternative framework when impairment/traits differ substantially from the six patterns, including mixed, atypical, or type-subthreshold presentations that still meet general PD requirements. The chapter represents Section II paranoid, schizoid, histrionic, and dependent presentations through PD-TS trait combinations rather than separate alternative types. It does not erase those Section II categories. (pp. 1158–1159)

## Specifiers & severity
No categorical subtypes are needed; specify the actual domains/facets and LPFS severity. Clinically relevant additional traits can be recorded with a matching named profile rather than automatically changing it to PD-TS. See [trait taxonomy](pathological-personality-traits.md). (pp. 1158–1160)

## Coding
PD-TS is an alternative-model formulation with no distinct ICD code supplied in this chapter. Do not automatically translate it into unspecified or other specified personality disorder; those clinical diagnoses require their Section II assessment and coding logic.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Identify general PD evidence and ≥2 impaired functioning areas before describing type-subthreshold or mixed features.
- Describe every elevated domain/facet, review of all five domains, data sources, developmental course, settings, functional effects, and exclusions.
- State why a named alternative profile does not fit and distinguish alternative formulation from any clinical diagnosis entered elsewhere.
