# Other Specified and Unspecified Bipolar and Related Disorder

> **Spark note:** These are residual categories for clinically significant bipolar-type presentations that don't meet full criteria for BP-I, BP-II, cyclothymia, or the induced or medical-condition forms. **Other specified (F31.89)** = you state *why* (e.g., "short-duration hypomanic episodes"). **Unspecified (F31.9)** = you don't, or can't yet (e.g., an ER visit with limited information). If you cannot decide between bipolar and depressive, use **unspecified mood disorder (F39)** ([unspecified-mood-disorder.md](unspecified-mood-disorder.md)).

## Other Specified Bipolar and Related Disorder: F31.89 (pp. 289–291)
**Requirements:** bipolar-type symptoms predominate and cause clinically significant distress or impairment, but full criteria for any specific disorder in the class are not met. The clinician chooses to state the reason.

**Recording:** "other specified bipolar and related disorder" + the reason (e.g., "short-duration cyclothymia").

**Example presentations:**
1. **Short-duration hypomanic episodes (2–3 days) and major depressive episodes**
   - ≥1 lifetime MDE.
   - Never met full criteria for a manic or hypomanic episode.
   - **≥2 episodes** of hypomania that meet **full symptom criteria** but last only **2–3 days**.
   - Hypomanic periods **do not overlap** with MDEs, so the picture is not an MDE with mixed features.
2. **Hypomanic episodes with insufficient symptoms and major depressive episodes**
   - ≥1 lifetime MDE; never full mania or hypomania.
   - ≥1 episode of subsyndromal hypomania lasting **≥4 consecutive days** with either:
     - elevated mood + **1–2** other hypomanic symptoms; **or**
     - irritable mood + **2–3** other symptoms.
   - No overlap with MDEs (so not mixed features).
3. **Hypomanic episode without prior major depressive episode**
   - ≥1 full hypomanic episode.
   - Never met criteria for an MDE or a manic episode.
   - This is also where cyclothymia goes if a hypomanic episode occurs after the initial window.
4. **Short-duration cyclothymia (<24 months)**
   - Multiple subthreshold hypomanic **and** subthreshold depressive periods lasting **<24 months (<12 months in youth)**.
   - Never met MDE, manic or hypomanic criteria; no psychotic disorder.
   - Symptoms on more days than not; no symptom-free gap >2 months; distress or impairment.
5. **Manic episode superimposed on schizophrenia, schizophreniform disorder, delusional disorder, or other specified/unspecified schizophrenia spectrum disorder**
   - **Note:** manic episodes that are part of **schizoaffective disorder do not** get an additional OSBRD diagnosis.

Section III also includes proposed criteria for a short-duration hypomania condition, intended for further research (chapter intro, p. 255).

## Unspecified Bipolar and Related Disorder: F31.9 (p. 291)
- Same threshold: bipolar-type symptoms predominate and cause distress or impairment, but full criteria are not met.
- Use it when the clinician **chooses not to specify** the reason, **or there is insufficient information** (e.g., **emergency room**).
- F31.9 is also the BP-I code for "current or most recent episode unspecified" and for "unspecified" severity/status. Make sure the label you write matches the diagnosis you intend.

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| **F31.89** | Other specified bipolar and related disorder + stated reason |
| **F31.9** | Unspecified bipolar and related disorder |
| F39 | Unspecified mood disorder: can't choose between unspecified bipolar and unspecified depressive (see separate file) |

## Differential diagnosis
- **BP-II:** requires a **full** hypomanic episode (≥4 days, full symptom count) **plus** a full MDE. Examples 1–2 fall short on duration or symptom count.
- **MDD:** subthreshold hypomanic symptoms can coexist with MDD. OSBRD examples 1–2 apply when there are discrete, non-overlapping hypomanic periods.
- **MDE with mixed features:** if opposite-pole symptoms overlap with the MDE, assess the full mixed-features threshold; overlap alone does not establish that specifier. The named short-hypomania examples require separate, non-overlapping periods.
- **Cyclothymic disorder:** needs ≥2 years (≥1 year in youth). Shorter → example 4.
- **Schizoaffective disorder:** mania within schizoaffective disorder → no OSBRD.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- **State the specific reason** for other specified (e.g., "2–3-day hypomanic periods ×3 with full symptom count; MDE ×2; never ≥4-day hypomania"). This is what separates F31.89 from F31.9.
- Record exact **duration** and **symptom counts** of each high period, and whether they **overlap** with depressive episodes (mixed-features check).
- Document a lifetime screen showing no full manic episode (and no full hypomanic episode for examples 1, 2 and 4).
- For **unspecified** use: note what information is missing and the plan to clarify (collateral, records, observation, reassessment once the person is no longer intoxicated).
- Rule out substance or medication and medical causes as for the specific disorders.
- Document distress or impairment in all cases.
