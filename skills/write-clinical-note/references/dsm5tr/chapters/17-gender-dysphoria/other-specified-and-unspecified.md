# Other Specified and Unspecified Gender Dysphoria

> **Spark note:** For distressing or impairing gender dysphoria symptoms that predominate but fall short of full criteria. Use **F64.8 other specified** with a stated reason; the TR's example is **brief gender dysphoria** (full criteria met but under 6 months). Use **F64.9 unspecified** when you don't state the reason or there isn't enough information.

## Criteria at a glance
- Symptoms characteristic of gender dysphoria.
- Clinically significant distress or impairment in social, occupational or other important areas.
- Predominate in the clinical picture.
- Full criteria for gender dysphoria not met.

## Coding
| Code | Use | Recording |
|---|---|---|
| F64.8 | Other specified gender dysphoria — reason given | "Other specified gender dysphoria, brief gender dysphoria" |
| F64.9 | Unspecified gender dysphoria — reason not given or insufficient info | "Unspecified gender dysphoria" |

## Example presentations
- **Brief gender dysphoria:** symptoms meet full criteria but duration is **<6 months** (p. 724).
- The source does not list additional examples. Other specified requires a characteristic dysphoria presentation and an explicit unmet requirement; distress about gender-nonconforming behavior or rejection alone does not establish that presentation. If information is insufficient and the reason is not specified, the unspecified category may be appropriate when its requirements are supported.

## Differential diagnosis
- Confirm full criteria aren't met for the child (≥6 of 8 incl. A1) or adolescent/adult (≥2 of 6) sets.
- Gender nonconformity without distress/impairment → no diagnosis.
- Consider transvestic disorder, BDD, ASD, psychosis as in the main files.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- State the gender-related symptoms, the distress/impairment, and the specific reason full criteria aren't met (e.g., "onset 3 months ago").
- Use client's name, pronouns and identity terms; record AFAB/AMAB.
- Suicide risk screen and support/victimization context as for full gender dysphoria.
- Plan to reassess duration/criteria at follow-up (e.g., after 6 months for brief presentations).
