# Schizotypal (Personality) Disorder

> **Spark note:** This disorder is **cross-listed** here because it belongs to the schizophrenia
> spectrum, and ICD-10 labels it "schizotypal disorder." Full criteria and text are in the
> **Personality Disorders** chapter. In this chapter, the point is that the odd beliefs, perceptions
> and thinking stay **below the psychotic threshold** and are part of an enduring personality pattern. (pp. 210, 212)

## Criteria at a glance
- Not given in this chapter. See the [full Personality Disorders note](../21-personality-disorders/schizotypal-personality-disorder.md) for the complete ≥5-of-9 criteria, exclusions, course and recording conventions.
- Chapter summary: a pervasive pattern of social and interpersonal deficits, including reduced capacity for
  close relationships, **cognitive or perceptual distortions** and **behavioral eccentricity**.
- Abnormal beliefs, thinking and perception are **subthreshold** for a psychotic disorder.

## Development & course
- Usually begins by early adulthood. Some cases first appear in childhood or adolescence (p. 210).
- Schizotypal or paranoid PD may **precede the onset of schizophrenia** (schizophrenia comorbidity, p. 232).
- Delusional disorder has a significant **familial link** with schizophrenia and schizotypal PD (p. 215).

## Differential diagnosis (from this chapter)
- **Schizophrenia:** schizotypal PD is distinguished by **subthreshold** symptoms tied to persistent personality features (p. 231).
- **Attenuated psychosis syndrome (F28 example):** psychotic-like symptoms below full threshold, but more transient, with insight relatively kept. Schizotypal PD is a trait pattern.
- Schizoid, paranoid and schizotypal PDs all appear in the differential for schizophreniform and schizoaffective disorder.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record a **longstanding** pattern from early adulthood (collateral, school or work history), not an episodic change from baseline.
- Note odd beliefs, magical thinking or ideas of reference held **without delusional conviction** (the person can consider alternatives).
- Note unusual perceptual experiences (e.g., sensing a presence) **without** full hallucinations. Note constricted or odd affect, eccentric dress or behavior, and lack of close confidants.
- Establish whether the enduring personality pattern is present independently of psychotic or mood episodes; use the full Personality Disorders exclusions and premorbid recording convention. A lifetime psychotic episode is not automatically an absolute exclusion of a premorbid schizotypal pattern. Assess medical/substance causes and ASD as applicable.
- MSE phrasing: "speech vague/overelaborate but goal-directed," "ideas of reference without fixed conviction," "no AH/VH endorsed," "affect constricted."
