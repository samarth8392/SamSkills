# Other Specified and Unspecified Paraphilic Disorder

> **Spark note:** These categories capture clinically consequential paraphilic presentations that do not meet a named disorder's full criteria. Other specified communicates the particular reason; unspecified leaves that reason unstated or reflects inadequate information. Neither category licenses diagnosing an atypical interest without significant distress/impairment. (pp. 1062–1063)

## Criteria at a glance
- Predominant paraphilic-disorder symptoms cause clinically significant distress or impaired functioning, while a specific disorder's complete criteria are not met.
- **Other specified:** clinician records the reason/focus after the diagnostic name.
- **Unspecified:** reason is not stated, including situations with insufficient diagnostic information. (pp. 1062–1063)

## Specifiers & severity
Other specified can carry remission and/or controlled-environment descriptors. This passage does not supply separate detailed remission thresholds for the residual category or extend these explicitly to unspecified. (p. 1063)

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| F65.89 | Other specified paraphilic disorder, followed by the reason/presentation. |
| F65.9 | Unspecified paraphilic disorder when the reason is unstated or information is insufficient. (pp. 1062–1063) |

## Diagnostic features & clinical pearls
Examples are persistent, intense arousal involving obscene telephone calls (telephone scatologia), corpses (necrophilia), animals (zoophilia), feces (coprophilia), enemas (klismaphilia), or urine (urophilia). The listed examples require ≥6 months and marked distress/impairment. The examples are nonexhaustive. (p. 1063)

## Suicide risk
Not specifically addressed.

## Differential diagnosis
- **Named paraphilic disorders:** first assess their particular focus, duration, consequence, and age rules.
- **Paraphilic interest without disorder:** atypical content alone does not establish the clinically significant consequence threshold.
- **Other specified versus unspecified:** the distinction is communication of the reason, not automatically severity. (pp. 1037, 1062–1063)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Describe actual reported/corroborated focus, duration, intensity, and functional consequences; record which named criteria are not met.
- For F65.89 append a reason rather than supplying only a broad residual label.
- For F65.9 identify the information gap and assessment plan where applicable; do not invent a focus to make the record appear specific.
- Assess consent/harm and relevant alternatives individually; do not equate fantasy content with an enacted offense.
