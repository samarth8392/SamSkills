# WHODAS 2.0 Printed Form and Item Domains

> **Spark note:** The two printed WHODAS pages are images in this PDF; their question text is absent from the chapter extraction. Visual inspection confirms the 36-item self-administered form, its six domains, clinician scoring columns and a **work/school applicability instruction**. This study map paraphrases functional areas; administer and score the actual instrument rather than this summary.

## Administration details visible on the form (pp. 1122–1123)

- Header fields identify the person, age and assessment date.
- Rate difficulty attributable to health or mental-health conditions during the **past 30 days**, considering disease, injury, emotional problems and alcohol/drug problems.
- Give one response to each applicable item. The usual response scale is **1 none, 2 mild, 3 moderate, 4 severe, 5 extreme/unable**.
- One participation item asks about **time occupied by the health condition or its effects** and uses amount-of-time anchors. Do not mechanically substitute difficulty wording for every question.
- Clinician columns allow entry of raw item scores, raw domain scores and domain averages. The bottom of the second page provides total and average general-disability fields.

| Mobility, D2.1–D2.5 | 5 | Standing about 30 minutes; rising from sitting; moving within the home; leaving home; walking a long distance, illustrated by about 1 km |
| Self-care, D3.1–D3.4 | 4 | Whole-body washing; dressing; eating; remaining alone for several days |
| Interpersonal functioning, D4.1–D4.5 | 5 | Interacting with unfamiliar people; keeping friendships; relationships with close others; forming friendships; sexual activity |
| Household activity, D5.1–D5.4 | 4 | Managing domestic responsibilities; quality of important tasks; completing required tasks; finishing at the required pace |
| Work/school activity, D5.5–D5.8 | 4 | Ordinary work/study demands; quality of important tasks; completing required work; working at the required pace |
| Participation, D6.1–D6.8 | 8 | Community participation; environmental barriers; others' effects on dignity; time occupied by illness; emotional effects; financial burden; family burden; independent leisure/relaxation |

- Six conceptual domains are represented, with **life activities split into household and work/school blocks** on the printed scoring form.
- The displayed full-form domain maxima are 30, 25, 20, 25, 20 for household, 20 for work/school and 40 for participation; the full-form raw total maximum is **180**. These are form displays, not impairment cutoffs.

## Work/school skip instruction (p. 1123)

- Complete D5.5–D5.8 when engaged in school or work, including paid, unpaid or self-employed work.
- Otherwise move directly from household items to D6.1. Record that the four work/school items were **not applicable under the form's instruction**.
- The form establishes this skip but does not itself explain an adjusted general-score denominator. The surrounding DSM scoring prose describes a 36-item total. These notes therefore do not invent an adjustment algorithm or equate an instructed skip with an accidental unanswered item. State applicability and scoring method, and consult the instrument's scoring guidance when deriving an adjusted score.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)

- Record the exact instrument/version and recall interval; confirm that difficulty was attributed to health conditions.
- Describe impairment with concrete functional examples from the relevant domain, keeping those examples separate from the instrument's actual item wording.
- Distinguish work/school skips, missing answers and clinician corrections. Preserve the source responses and state the method used for totals or averages.
- See [WHODAS description and scoring](whodas-2.0.md) for the book's narrative scoring guidance and missing-data cautions.
