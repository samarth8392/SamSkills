# Conditions for Further Study

These seven Section III proposals supply research definitions. The source explicitly says they are not intended for clinical diagnostic use and lack sufficient evidence for official Section II inclusion. Do not present their thresholds as billable clinical diagnoses.

## High-yield threshold map
| Proposal | Defining time/count rule |
|---|---|
| Attenuated psychosis | ≥1 attenuated positive symptom ≥weekly for 1 month, new/worse within 1 year; never a psychotic disorder |
| Depression with short hypomania | ≥1 MDE plus ≥2 hypomanic periods of ≥2 but <4 days, meeting other proposed symptom/function requirements |
| Caffeine use | All first 3 criteria (failed control, continued harmful use, withdrawal) within 12 months plus impairment/distress |
| Internet gaming | ≥5/9 within 12 months plus impairment/distress; hours alone insufficient |
| ND-PAE | Exposure + ≥1 neurocognitive + ≥1 self-regulatory + ≥2 adaptive deficits, including communication/social interaction; childhood onset |
| Suicidal behavior | Initiated attempt within 24 months, rather than ideation/preparation |
| NSSI | Injury on ≥5 days in past year plus associated functions/antecedents, distress/interference, and exclusions |

## TR changes and coding distinctions
Persistent complex bereavement disorder moved out of further study, was reformulated, and is now **prolonged grief disorder**, an official Section II trauma/stressor-related diagnosis. Do not retain its older research status. (p. 1168)

Suicidal behavior and NSSI now have source-listed clinically usable behavior/history codes. Those Section II focus-of-attention entries are distinct from these research-disorder proposals. Gaming's ICD-11 recognition also does not make this DSM proposal a Section II diagnosis. No shared severity or remission rule applies to all seven proposals.

## Chapter-level differential logic
Assess symptoms, functioning, and safety directly. Recent attenuated psychosis differs from longstanding schizotypal traits; brief activation differs from full mania/hypomania; caffeine dependence-like physiology differs from harmful failed-control use; gaming engagement differs from impairment. Prenatal exposure requires documented developmental deficits. Suicide intent must be evaluated for each act separately from NSSI history.
