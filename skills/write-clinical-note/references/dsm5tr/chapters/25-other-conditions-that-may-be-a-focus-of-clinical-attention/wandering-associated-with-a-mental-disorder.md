# Wandering Associated With a Mental Disorder

> **Spark note:** Use for a wandering drive associated with a mental disorder that creates significant safety or clinical-management concerns. Record the underlying disorder first. (p. 1101)

## Clinical use and differential
Examples include major neurocognitive and neurodevelopmental disorders in which wandering risks falls or departure from needed supervision. Exclude purposeful escape from unwanted housing/hospitalization and walking/pacing due to medication-induced akathisia. (p. 1101)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Describe observed/reported wandering, associated disorder, supervision needs, and actual assessed safety consequences.
- Establish the behavior's purpose and assess medication-related restlessness before applying the category.
- Record the associated mental disorder followed by Z91.83 and the specific association in words.
