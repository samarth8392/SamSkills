# Uncomplicated Bereavement

> **Spark note:** Normal grief can warrant care and include sadness, insomnia, appetite loss, and weight change. Those symptoms alone do not establish a depressive episode; assess major depression and prolonged grief disorder separately. (p. 1101)

## Clinical use and differential
Expression and duration vary greatly across cultures. A grieving individual may view the mood response as normal while seeking relief for associated symptoms. This subsection refers the reader to the MDE and prolonged-grief texts for their distinguishing features; it does not supply their criteria. (p. 1101)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record loss, timeline, cultural context, symptoms/function, and reason for clinical attention.
- Explain the assessed basis for uncomplicated grief and any indicated separate disorder evaluation; do not assume grief precludes major depression.
