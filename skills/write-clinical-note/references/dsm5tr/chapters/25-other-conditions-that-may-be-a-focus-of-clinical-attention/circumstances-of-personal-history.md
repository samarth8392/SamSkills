# Circumstances of Personal History

> **Spark note:** A clinically relevant history can be recorded even without a current disorder. Trauma or deployment history alone does not establish PTSD or any other mental diagnosis. (p. 1100)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record the reported historical circumstance, source, and why it informs current care; obtain only clinically needed detail.
- Distinguish past deployment from current deployment-status occupational problems (Z56.82).
- Where the text gives more specific abuse-history categories, preserve the specific documented context rather than obscuring it in a broad label. The supplied subsection itself contains no additional diagnostic criteria or thresholds.
