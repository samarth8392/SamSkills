# Problems Related to the Social Environment

> **Spark note:** These codes describe clinically relevant social circumstances, including reported discrimination. Living alone or membership in a cultural/minority group is not itself a pathology. (pp. 1098–1099)

## Clinical use and distinctions
- **Living alone:** concerns such as loneliness, isolation, or loss of daily structure that affect care; solitary residence alone is insufficient.
- **Acculturation:** difficulty adapting to a new culture, including after migration.
- **Exclusion/rejection:** recurrent exclusion in a setting with social-power imbalance, including bullying or intimidation.
- **Adverse discrimination/persecution:** perceived or experienced targeting based on actual or perceived group membership, including gender/identity, race, ethnicity, religion, orientation, nationality, politics, disability, caste/status, weight, or appearance. Do not infer a delusion merely from the label's word “perceived.” (pp. 1098–1099)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Attribute experiences to the reporting source; describe behaviors/context, frequency, power imbalance where relevant, and clinical impact.
- Assess cultural context and available support; distinguish a circumstance from any separately established mental disorder.
- Use a specific category when supported and describe the problem when using Z60.9.
