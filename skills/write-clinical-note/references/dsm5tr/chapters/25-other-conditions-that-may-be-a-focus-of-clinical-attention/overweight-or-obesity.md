# Overweight or Obesity

> **Spark note:** This subsection supplies a clinical-attention code for overweight/obesity, not a mental-disorder diagnosis or a diagnostic BMI algorithm. (p. 1102)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record the established/reported weight-related condition and why it is receiving clinical attention; distinguish measured findings from report.
- Do not infer binge-eating disorder, nonadherence, or causation from body size. Document any separately assessed eating disorder or medication issue on its own evidence.
