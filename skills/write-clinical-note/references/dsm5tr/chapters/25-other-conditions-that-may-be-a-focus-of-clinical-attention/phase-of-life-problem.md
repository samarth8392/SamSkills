# Phase of Life Problem

> **Spark note:** A life transition can be a clinical focus without meeting mental-disorder criteria. Examples span schooling, independence, marriage, career, parenthood, children leaving home, and retirement. (p. 1101)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Identify the transition and specific adjustment difficulty and explain its effect on treatment/prognosis or why it is the visit focus.
- Assess symptoms and impairment independently if adjustment disorder or another diagnosis is suspected; the transition alone establishes neither.
