# Economic Problems

> **Spark note:** Identify the concrete resource shortage and why it matters clinically rather than inferring poverty from appearance or a missed appointment. Food and safe water have separate codes. (p. 1098)

## Clinical use and distinctions
Insufficient insurance/welfare support includes eligible individuals not receiving support, benefits insufficient for needs, and barriers to needed programs (for example documentation/address obstacles or eligibility restrictions). The chapter does not supply numerical income cutoffs for low income/extreme poverty. (p. 1098)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record the specific reported resource deficit, access/eligibility barrier, and effect on treatment, medication, nutrition, or prognosis as assessed.
- Distinguish lack of resources from unwillingness to follow treatment; assess both if supported.
- Specify the economic label with Z59.9 because that code also denotes other housing problems in this source.
