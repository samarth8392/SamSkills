# Occupational Problems

> **Spark note:** Code the work circumstance that is receiving attention or affecting care/prognosis. Deployment status is an occupational context; a psychological response to deployment is assessed as adjustment disorder or another disorder. (pp. 1096–1097)

## Clinical use and distinctions
Specify job loss versus threatened loss, job transition, schedule, interpersonal conflict, environmental strain, or harassment. Career uncertainty and other work difficulties may also warrant attention; use the appropriate broad category when a more specific listed label does not fit. (pp. 1096–1097)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record work/deployment circumstance, chronology, and its concrete effect on functioning, attendance, sleep, treatment access, or prognosis when assessed.
- Distinguish current deployment context from personal deployment history (Z91.82) and from a separately established stress-related disorder.
- Attribute harassment/conflict accounts and document the clinical relevance without assuming unverified personnel or legal findings.
