# Counseling and Medical Advice Encounters

> **Spark note:** These codes describe the service being sought, not an assumed disorder. Sexual orientation, questions about sex, or a request for genetic-risk advice are not themselves diagnoses. (p. 1100)

## Clinical use and distinctions
- **Genetic counseling:** understanding familial/genetic mental-disorder risks for oneself, relatives, existing children, or future children.
- **Sex counseling:** education, behavior, enjoyment, orientation, attitudes, or concerns about others' sexual behavior/orientation.
- **Dietary counseling:** dietary concerns such as weight management.
- **Other counseling/consultation:** advice not otherwise specified in the chapter; the source gives adolescent substance-abuse-prevention counseling as an example. (p. 1100)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- State the individual's question, actual counseling focus, information provided, and assessed follow-up needs.
- Do not infer a genetic diagnosis, sexual dysfunction, eating disorder, or substance disorder merely from the counseling encounter.
