# Religious or Spiritual Problem

> **Spark note:** Loss/questioning of faith, conversion-related difficulties, or conflicts about spiritual values can warrant attention without being mental disorders or requiring membership in an organized religion. (p. 1101)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Describe the individual's stated concern, meaning, cultural/spiritual context, and reason for care.
- Avoid equating unfamiliar beliefs with psychopathology; evaluate any suspected mental disorder independently.
