# Nonadherence to Medical Treatment

> **Spark note:** Use only when difficulty following an important treatment element is severe enough to merit independent attention and does not meet criteria for psychological factors affecting other medical conditions. Assess why it occurs before labeling it. (p. 1102)

## Clinical use and differential
Possible contributors include adverse effects, costs, personal values, religious/cultural beliefs, age-related limitations, or a mental disorder. It may concern psychiatric or other medical treatment. The subsection supplies no frequency threshold and does not restrict the category to intentional refusal. (p. 1102)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Specify the treatment element, actual pattern, source, consequences, and independent clinical need.
- Document barriers, understanding, preferences, side effects, and any mental/cognitive contributors when assessed.
- Establish that psychological factors affecting other medical conditions does not better account for the presentation; do not infer motivation from a missed dose/visit.
