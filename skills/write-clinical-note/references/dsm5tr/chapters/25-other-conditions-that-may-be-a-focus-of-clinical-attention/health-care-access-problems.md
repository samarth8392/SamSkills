# Problems Related to Access to Medical and Other Health Care

> **Spark note:** Document access barriers when they are the clinical focus or affect treatment/prognosis. A service being unavailable differs from choosing not to use accessible care. (p. 1100)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Identify the needed facility/agency, reported access obstacle, and effect on care or prognosis.
- Distinguish service availability from insurance/benefit insufficiency (Z59.7) and independently assess nonadherence when relevant.
- Describe actual assessed barriers and referral needs; do not assume that a missed visit reflects refusal.
