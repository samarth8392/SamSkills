# Age-Related Cognitive Decline

> **Spark note:** The decline must be objectively identified and remain within normal limits for age. Subjective memory complaints alone do not settle this category, and neurological or specific mental-disorder explanations must be excluded. (pp. 1102–1103)

## Clinical use and differential
Examples include trouble recalling names/appointments or solving complex problems. Before applying the category, evaluate alternative explanations: a mental diagnosis or neurological disease must not account for the change. Assess neurocognitive disorders separately. (pp. 1102–1103)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record the reported change, objective evidence, age-based interpretation, and functional context.
- Describe relevant medical/neurological and mental-disorder assessment and uncertainty; do not assert normal aging from age alone.
