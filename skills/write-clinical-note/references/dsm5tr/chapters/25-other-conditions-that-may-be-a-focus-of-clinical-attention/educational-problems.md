# Educational Problems

> **Spark note:** Educational context deserves separate documentation when it is the clinical focus or changes diagnosis, care, or prognosis. Low achievement can reflect access, instruction, conflict, or an individual disorder; the code alone does not determine the cause. (p. 1096)

## Clinical use and distinctions
Choose the specific educational difficulty. Underachievement refers to performance below expectations given intellectual capacity; lack of opportunity and inadequate teaching are distinct from failed examinations or interpersonal discord. The supplied text gives no duration or test-score threshold. (p. 1096)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Identify literacy/access, test/grade performance, instructional limitations, or school conflict using reported/recorded evidence.
- Describe effects on symptoms, assessment validity, treatment understanding, or prognosis.
- Assess suspected learning, attention, intellectual, or other disorders separately; an educational code is not proof of a neurodevelopmental diagnosis.
