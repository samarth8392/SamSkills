# Problems Related to Interaction With the Legal System

> **Spark note:** Legal circumstances can be the clinical focus or alter care/prognosis without constituting a mental disorder. Select the actual legal context rather than treating all legal involvement as incarceration. (p. 1099)

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Record reported/verified legal status, timeline, relevant restrictions or barriers, and the reason it affects treatment or prognosis.
- Separate a client's account, collateral information, and court documentation; do not imply a conviction or guilt from pending proceedings.
- Assess psychological reactions independently. A medicolegal context alone does not establish malingering.
