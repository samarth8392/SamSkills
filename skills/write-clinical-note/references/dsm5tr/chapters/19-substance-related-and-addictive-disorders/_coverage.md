## Scope and honest limitations
- New notes paraphrase thresholds, timing, exclusions, coding/specifiers, clinical differential and documentation cues from the extracted source. Minor residual/cross-reference sections are intentionally shorter than major disorder notes.
- Existing notes were retained with targeted YAML/coding-scope corrections; this pass does not certify every pre-existing sentence or every line of historical epidemiology against a visual PDF image.
- The extraction was the reading source. No page-image/layout audit, independent clinical validation, current billing-code verification, or formal DSM-5 versus TR comparison was performed. The chapter's extracted overview table has imperfect layout; individual criteria/sections were preferred for precise decisions.
- Prevalence numbers retain their original population, time and diagnostic context; exposure rates and symptom-only rates are not automatically disorder prevalence.
- Documentation cues are prompts for recording evidence actually obtained, not invented patient observations. Missing full induced-syndrome criteria are intentionally linked to their phenomenological chapters rather than reconstructed here.
