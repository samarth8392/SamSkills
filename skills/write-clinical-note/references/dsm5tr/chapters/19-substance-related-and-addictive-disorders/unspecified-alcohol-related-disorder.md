# Unspecified Alcohol-Related Disorder

> **Spark note:** A residual category. Alcohol-related symptoms cause clinically significant distress or impairment and **predominate**, but full criteria are not met for any specific alcohol-related disorder or any other disorder in the substance-related and addictive class. There is no "other specified" version for alcohol, so this is the only residual code.

## Criteria at a glance (p. 784)
- Symptoms characteristic of an alcohol-related disorder.
- They cause clinically significant distress or impairment in social, occupational or other areas.
- They predominate in the clinical picture.
- They do **not** meet full criteria for any specific alcohol-related disorder or any substance-related and addictive disorder.

## Coding
| Code | Use when |
|---|---|
| F10.99 | Unspecified alcohol-related disorder |

## Clinical pearls
- Typical uses: only **one** AUD criterion (below the threshold of 2) but impairing; a withdrawal-like picture with too few signs; induced symptoms whose attribution is uncertain; or insufficient information, e.g., in the ED.
- Reassess once more history is available. Upgrade to a specific diagnosis when criteria are met.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- State **which alcohol-related symptoms** are present and the resulting **distress or impairment**.
- State **which specific disorder was considered and why criteria were not met** (e.g., "only 1 of 11 AUD criteria endorsed: hazardous use (DUI)").
- Note any plans to gather collateral or labs to clarify.
