# Opioid-Induced Mental Disorders

> **Spark note:** Pointer entry. Opioids can induce **depressive, anxiety, sleep and sexual** disorders and **delirium** (intoxication, withdrawal, or as prescribed). Criteria live in those chapters. Diagnose them **instead of** plain intoxication/withdrawal **only when symptoms are severe enough to warrant independent clinical attention.**

## Where the criteria live (p. 846)
| Disorder | Chapter | Onset specifier available (Table 1, p. 755) |
|---|---|---|
| Opioid-induced depressive disorder | Depressive Disorders | I/W |
| Opioid-induced anxiety disorder | Anxiety Disorders | W |
| Opioid-induced sleep disorder | Sleep-Wake Disorders | I/W |
| Opioid-induced sexual dysfunction | Sexual Dysfunctions | (column truncated in source text) |
| Opioid intoxication delirium; opioid withdrawal delirium; opioid (taken as prescribed)-induced delirium | Neurocognitive Disorders | (column truncated in source text) |

Table 1 lists no opioid-induced psychotic, bipolar or OCD-related disorder. In the extracted text, the sexual-dysfunction, delirium and NCD columns of Table 1 are cut off, so check the printed table for those onset specifiers.

## Coding
- Codes are given in the target chapter. The 4th character carries OUD status: F11.1x mild OUD, F11.2x moderate/severe OUD, F11.9x no OUD. E.g., F11.14 / F11.24 opioid-induced depressive disorder with mild / moderate-severe OUD (p. 834).
- When an induced disorder is coded, don't separately code OUD.

## Differential & pearls
- Versus intoxication/withdrawal: the induced syndrome is **in excess** of what usually accompanies intoxication or withdrawal, **predominates**, and needs its own clinical attention.
- Versus independent disorder (general rule, p. 762): it preceded use, or persists >~1 month after acute withdrawal or severe intoxication, which points to independent.
- Opioids are less likely than most drugs of abuse to produce mental disturbance (p. 840).

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Timeline: symptom onset relative to opioid start, escalation, intoxication or withdrawal, and any prior abstinent periods (were symptoms present then?).
- Why the symptoms exceed the expected syndrome (severity, duration, need for specific treatment).
- Planned reassessment after ~1 month of abstinence to confirm induced versus independent.
- Specify onset during intoxication or withdrawal, and OUD severity for the 4th character.
