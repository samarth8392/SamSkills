# Unspecified Opioid-Related Disorder

> **Spark note:** Residual category (**F11.99**) for clinically significant opioid-related symptoms that predominate but don't meet full criteria for any specific opioid-related disorder or any other disorder in the substance-related and addictive class.

## Criteria at a glance
- Symptoms characteristic of an opioid-related disorder cause clinically significant distress/impairment and predominate.
- Full criteria are **not** met for OUD, intoxication, withdrawal, an opioid-induced mental disorder, or any other disorder in the class.

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| F11.99 | Unspecified opioid-related disorder |

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Name the opioid and describe the symptoms and impairment.
- State which specific diagnoses were considered and which criteria were missing (e.g., only 1 OUD criterion; withdrawal with only 2 Criterion-B signs).
- Plan to re-evaluate as more history emerges.
