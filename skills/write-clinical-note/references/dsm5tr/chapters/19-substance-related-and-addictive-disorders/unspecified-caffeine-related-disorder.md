# Unspecified Caffeine-Related Disorder

> **Spark note:** A residual code for impairing caffeine-related symptoms that do not meet criteria for caffeine intoxication, caffeine withdrawal, a caffeine-induced disorder, or any other substance-related and addictive disorder. Examples: only 4 intoxication signs, or 2 withdrawal symptoms, with clear impairment. A problematic **pattern** of caffeine use (a possible "caffeine use disorder") has no formal diagnosis in the main text. This category, or the Conditions for Further Study material, is the closest fit.

## Criteria at a glance (p. 791)
- Symptoms characteristic of a caffeine-related disorder **predominate** and cause clinically significant distress or impairment.
- Full criteria for any specific caffeine-related disorder, or any disorder in the class, are **not** met.

## Coding
| Code | Use when |
|---|---|
| F15.99 | Unspecified caffeine-related disorder |

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Name the symptoms and the impairment, and quantify caffeine intake.
- State which specific diagnosis was considered and the shortfall (e.g., "4 of 5 required intoxication signs").
- Note the temporal link to caffeine intake or cessation.
