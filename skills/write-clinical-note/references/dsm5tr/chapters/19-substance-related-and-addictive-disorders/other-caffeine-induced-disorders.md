# Caffeine-Induced Mental Disorders

> **Spark note:** Only **two** caffeine-induced disorders exist: **caffeine-induced anxiety disorder** (onset during intoxication) and **caffeine-induced sleep disorder** (onset during intoxication or withdrawal). Diagnose them instead of caffeine intoxication or withdrawal only when anxiety or sleep symptoms are severe enough to need independent clinical attention. There is no caffeine use disorder, so codes are always the "without use disorder" form.

## Which disorders, and where the criteria live (p. 791)
| Induced disorder | Criteria in chapter | Onset specifiers possible | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caffeine-induced anxiety disorder | Anxiety Disorders | During intoxication | F15.980 |
| Caffeine-induced sleep disorder | Sleep-Wake Disorders | During intoxication or withdrawal | F15.982 |

## Criteria at a glance
- Follows the general substance/medication-induced framework (see `_overview-part1.md`): symptoms predominate; they arise during or soon after intoxication or withdrawal; caffeine can produce them; no independent disorder explains them (no prior onset, no persistence ≥~1 month); not only during delirium; distress or impairment.
- **Threshold:** symptoms **exceed** those usual for caffeine intoxication (anxiety, insomnia) or withdrawal (sleep change), **predominate**, and **warrant independent attention**.

## Differential diagnosis
- **Caffeine intoxication:** anxiety and insomnia are part of the usual ≥5-sign picture.
- **Caffeine withdrawal:** sleep changes are within the expected course (2–9 days).
- **Panic disorder / GAD / insomnia disorder:** symptoms precede caffeine use or persist after abstinence. Note that people with anxiety disorders tend to **avoid** caffeine.

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Quantify caffeine intake and its temporal link to symptom onset.
- Explain **why the symptoms exceed** the usual intoxication or withdrawal picture (e.g., recurrent panic attacks, multi-night insomnia with daytime impairment).
- Document the response to reducing or stopping caffeine. Reassess if symptoms persist.
- Code F15.980 (anxiety) or F15.982 (sleep) and note the onset specifier.
