# Unspecified Sedative-, Hypnotic-, or Anxiolytic-Related Disorder

> **Spark note:** **F13.99** residual category: clinically significant sedative-related symptoms predominate but full criteria are not met for any specific SHA-related disorder or any other disorder in the class.

## Criteria at a glance
- Symptoms characteristic of a SHA-related disorder; clinically significant distress/impairment; predominate.
- Full criteria not met for any specific SHA-related disorder or any other substance-related/addictive disorder.

## Coding
| Code | Use |
|---|---|
| F13.99 | Unspecified sedative-, hypnotic-, or anxiolytic-related disorder |

## Documentation cues (for client notes)
- Agent, pattern of use, and the specific symptoms and impairment.
- Which disorders were considered and which thresholds were not met (e.g., withdrawal with only 1 Criterion B sign; 1 use-disorder criterion).
- Re-evaluation plan.
