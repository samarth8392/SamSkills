## Limitations and unresolved source issues
- Feigning and psychosis differential descriptions are group-level clinical tendencies, not validated individual tests. Notes avoid treating a dramatic presentation, gain context, voice location, or hypnotizability as dispositive.
- Genetic estimates are for dissociative symptoms, not proof of a particular diagnosis. Medical workup needs depend on presentation; normal orientation alone cannot establish a dissociative etiology.
