# DSM-5-TR source coverage audit

Audit date: 2026-10-15. Source: `references/source/DSM 5 TR-APA (2022).pdf`, 8,873,813 bytes, **1,377 PDF pages**. All page numbers below refer to the PDF, not the book's printed pagination.

## Findings and repairs

- The initial 33 chapter extractions contained **1,256 unique page markers**, with no duplicate markers. **121 pages were omitted** because the extraction script selected level-2 outline entries plus Classification and excluded other level-1 material.
- Seven new `supplement-*.md` files recover those 121 pages. Validation after supplementation found **40 extracted files, 1,377 markers, 1,377 distinct pages, no missing page numbers and no duplicates**.
- Page coverage is now complete. This means every PDF page has a marked extraction location; it does **not** mean every image, table layout or diagnostic statement has been transcribed or independently validated.
- WHODAS pages **1122–1123** contain the only substantive full-page image forms found in the PDF. They were visually inspected after rendering. A new paraphrased [item-domain and administration note](chapters/26-assessment-measures/whodas-printed-form-and-item-domains.md) addresses their content and the work/school skip instruction.
- New [preface notes](chapters/01-section-i-basics/prefaces-and-revision-scope.md) and [section-opening notes](chapters/01-section-i-basics/section-introductions-and-clinical-status.md) capture substantive material previously outside the extracted chapter boundaries.

## Close-reading scope and corrections

**Complete source reads by this audit agent:** chapter 13 (543–575), 14 (576–585), 17 (712–725), 18 (726–752), 23 (1064–1067), 24 (1068–1083) and 26 (1107–1123): **121 PDF pages in seven chapters**, including both WHODAS images by visual inspection. Source text was read in successive non-overlapping chunks; comparisons included criteria, coding, course, differential, functional consequences and risk information in the existing notes. Prefaces (35–40) and section openings (92–93, 126–129, 1104–1106) were also read to author the new orientation notes. Full-source review of chapters 16 and 21 was delegated by the parent to other existing agents; this agent's coverage of those two chapters remains structural. The parent separately reviewed complete Section I source files 01–03. Administrative lists and the subject Index were recovered and structurally inspected, not individually summarized or clinically close-read.

The following findings were corrected during this audit or by the parent in response to it:

1. **Orientation and revision scope:** new preface/section-opening notes capture the more-than-70 criteria clarifications, three editorial processes, added grief diagnosis and symptom codes, and distinctions among approved diagnoses, contextual conditions, assessment tools and research proposals.
2. **Section I interpretation:** the parent qualified provisional diagnoses with the source's strong-presumption/upper-duration rules; removed the invented numeric near-threshold vignette while preserving the actual clinical-judgment allowance; and narrowed the Section III other-specified statement to the convention described on p. 115.
3. **Chapter 13:** removed unverified post-2022 coding-expansion claims from the overview and AN/BN/BED notes. Qualified overview shortcuts that treated low weight plus bingeing/purging as sufficient for AN. Preserved the full AN requirements and distinguished BED's typical lack of sustained restriction from an additional formal exclusion. Clarified that normal-range BMI alone does not establish atypical AN.
4. **Chapter 14:** replaced a low-frequency-enuresis documentation example that could ignore Criterion B's alternative distress/impairment route. The source itself lists low-frequency enuresis as a residual example; the note now preserves that example while explaining the tension with the full criteria rather than inventing a new threshold.
5. **Chapter 17:** added the source's absence-of-general-population-outcome-data caveat to childhood persistence figures. Removed an unsupported claim about what largely causes suicide risk and an invented residual-category example that could blur gender nonconformity with diagnostic dysphoria.
6. **Chapter 18:** clarified that IED's verbal-aggression pathway does not require physical assault; preserved the source's qualification about abuse-related running away; and marked unplanned kleptomania theft as a descriptive tendency rather than an added absolute criterion.
7. **Chapter 23:** corrected suggestions that incomplete evaluation by itself warrants F99, and that residual diagnoses automatically take the formal provisional modifier. Clinically significant mental-disorder evidence remains necessary.
8. **Chapter 24:** corrected tardive dystonia/akathisia wording that made potential prolonged persistence sound like a mandatory minimum duration; distinguished failure to express distress in catatonia from proof of absent distress; and qualified automatic stimulant-withdrawal coding after stopping stimulant augmentation.
9. **Chapter 26:** visually inspected and mapped all 36 WHODAS functional areas, scoring fields and the work/school skip instruction. The parent qualified general-score missing-data guidance and linked the form supplement. Removed an implication that a child-rated Level 2 suicide measure exists; distinguished domain-specific equivocal psychosis-scale anchors from a diagnosis of attenuated psychosis syndrome; and corrected blanket item-count/recall wording for the parent form.

**Source ambiguities retained:** p. 746's conduct-disorder differential says stressor-linked problems “do not resolve within 6 months,” conflicting with the adjustment-disorder rule and the earlier ODD differential; the existing conduct-disorder note explicitly flags that source inconsistency. The WHODAS form permits a work/school skip, while the adjacent narrative describes dividing the complete total by 36 without explaining an adjusted denominator. The new form note records this limit and does not invent a scoring algorithm. These ambiguities are not silently treated as verified clinical instructions.

Existing local links in the assigned chapter files were checked. Some links to folders being authored concurrently were unresolved during the audit; final index/link verification should run after all agents finish. The new form note and Section I supplements must be included in that final pass.

## Limits of this audit

This audit establishes source-page coverage, explains extraction boundaries, restores missing raw sections, inventories assigned study-note categories and includes complete source rereads with targeted corrections in seven assigned chapters. It does **not** certify all notes as a clinically validated substitute for the source, audit current billing codes, or claim a sentence-by-sentence review of 1,377 pages. Chapters assigned to other agents were not redundantly reviewed here. Exact questionnaire administration should continue to use the printed instrument, not paraphrased study notes.
